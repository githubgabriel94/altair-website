1. Who we are

This website and our apps are operated by Altair Solutions Inc. (“Altair Solutions”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

Our approach is simple: we collect as little as we can, we don't sell personal information, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts or fonts, and it has no forms or accounts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, which it uses to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details. We don't receive or use that information to identify visitors.

3. Our apps

Each of our apps is designed to work with as little personal information as possible. Unless an app's own section below says otherwise:

  • the app does not require an account;
  • content you create in the app is stored on your device (and, if you use it, in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access);
  • the app does not contain third-party advertising or tracking;
  • we don't use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If an app asks for a permission (for example, your location or photos), it asks at the moment it needs it, explains why, and keeps working as far as possible if you say no. You can change permissions anytime in your device's Settings.

Purchases. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics from Apple. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated usage statistics and crash reports that do not identify you. We use them only to fix problems and improve our apps.

Each app's App Store page also includes an “App Privacy” summary.

4. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to provide support, and we keep it only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only when it's needed to provide a service you asked for (for example, with an email provider that delivers our messages), when required by law, or with your permission.

6. Children

Our website and apps are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Because most app data stays on your device, deleting an app also deletes the data it stored there.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the affected app.
