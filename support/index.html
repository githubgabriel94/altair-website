When you write to us

To help us help you quickly, please include:

  • the name of the app and its version (you'll find it in the app's settings or in the App Store),
  • your device model and its iOS, iPadOS or macOS version,
  • what happened, and what you expected to happen.

We're a small team, so replies can take a few business days.

Purchases, refunds and subscriptions

Purchases made through the App Store are processed by Apple. To request a refund or manage a subscription, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.
